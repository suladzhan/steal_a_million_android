collapses the animation root differently from the model and copied Humanoid
avatars produce incorrect body offsets. Keep imported file scale at 1; the
centimetre armature transform is intentional. BakeMesh measurements require
`useScale=true`. The avatar reference aligns arms and legs before mapping.
