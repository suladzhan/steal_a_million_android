STEAL A MILLION stores game progress, settings and unlocked cosmetic content locally
on your device. The game does not require an account. In this version, we do not
operate a game server and the app does not send personal information or gameplay
analytics to us. No third-party advertising, analytics, account or payment SDK is
included in the release build.

The operating system and Google Play may process information independently under
their own policies. Opening a linked policy page uses your browser and the website's
network connection.

You can reset game progress in the game's Settings. You can remove the app's locally
stored data using Android's app settings or by uninstalling the app. Android/device
backup behavior may depend on your operating system settings.

We do not collect contact details from the game. If you separately contact us for
support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to you.
Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

All money, odds and rewards in the game are fictional gameplay values. There are no
real-money payouts or financial accounts.

If the app's data practices change, the published policy and Google Play Data Safety
information will be updated. Contact the publisher at the address above for questions.
